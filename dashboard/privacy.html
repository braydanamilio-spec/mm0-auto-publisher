Privacy Policy

MM0 Publisher Console · last updated 26 August 2026

In short. This is a private tool used by its own operator to publish their own videos. It connects to Google Drive and YouTube accounts that the operator owns, stores the videos it produces in those accounts, and uploads them to those channels. It does not sell, rent, or share personal data with third parties, and it shows no advertising.

Who this applies to

MM0 Publisher Console (“the app”) is operated privately. The only people who authorise it are the operator and accounts belonging to the operator. There is no public sign-up.

What Google data the app accesses

When you authorise the app, it requests these Google OAuth scopes:

ScopeWhy it is needed
.../auth/drive Create a folder in your Drive, upload finished videos, thumbnails and their metadata files into it, read them back when publishing, and delete old files during cleanup.
.../auth/youtube.upload Upload finished videos to the YouTube channel you connected.
.../auth/userinfo.email Read your account email so connected accounts can be told apart in the dashboard.

What the app stores

StoredPurpose
Account email addressLabel the connection in the dashboard
OAuth refresh tokenKeep access working without asking you to sign in repeatedly
OAuth client id and secret used for that connectionRefresh the token with the same client that issued it
Drive folder id, storage capacity and amount usedDecide which storage account has room for the next upload
Connection timestampDiagnostics and cleanup

The app does not read, index, or copy files in your Drive that it did not create, and it does not collect contacts, calendar, location, browsing history, or payment information.

Where it is stored

  • Google Cloud Firestore — the primary record of connected accounts.
  • Cloudflare Workers KV and D1 — a short-lived cache so the pipeline keeps working when Firestore is rate-limited. Same data, no additional fields.

Access is restricted to the operator’s own credentials. Tokens are never displayed in the dashboard and are never sent to a browser.

What is never shared

No personal data is sold, rented, or shared with advertisers, data brokers, or any third party. The app does contact a small number of public media sources — Pexels, Pixabay, DVIDS and the U.S. National Archives — but only to download openly licensed images and footage. No user data is sent to them.

Google user data and limited use

The app’s use of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements. Google user data is used only to provide the features described above. It is not used for advertising, is not sold, and is not used to train generalised machine-learning models.

How to revoke access and delete your data

  • In the dashboard, open Storage or API Connections and press Disconnect on the account. This revokes the refresh token with Google and deletes the stored connection record.
  • You can also revoke access at any time at myaccount.google.com/permissions.
  • Video files already written into your own Drive stay in your Drive; you control them directly. Cleanup performed by the app moves files to your Drive trash, where Google keeps them for 30 days before permanent deletion.

Retention

Connection records are kept for as long as the account stays connected. When you disconnect, the record is deleted and the token is revoked. Cached copies expire automatically.

Children

The app is not directed at children and is not available for public sign-up.

Changes

If this policy changes, the date at the top of this page is updated. Material changes that affect how Google user data is handled will be reflected here before they take effect.
